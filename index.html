Privacy Policy — Wazin

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

Wazin (وازن) is an Android app for small businesses that records daily income and shows where it goes (costs, workers, personal savings boxes). It is published by TechTome. Contact: techtome14@gmail.com.

The short version

The app keeps your business records on your phone. We do not have your data. The app has no account, no server and no advertising, and it does not send your records to us or to anyone else.

What data the app handles

Everything you type in the app (business name, currency, costs, workers' names and pay, services, personal boxes, daily income, attendance, payments and your history) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not included in Android's cloud backup. It never leaves the phone unless you choose to move it yourself, as described below. We cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

What you can choose to move off the phone

  • Backup: you can save a backup file or share it (for example to Downloads, e-mail, a messaging app, Drive or a USB drive). You choose where it goes; after that, the place you chose is responsible for it. Anyone who gets the file can read your records, so keep it safe.
  • Export: you can save your history as a CSV file in the same way.
  • Automatic backups: if you switch them on, copies are kept in the app's own storage on the phone and, if you pick a folder, copied to that folder. Uninstalling the app deletes the copies stored in the app's own storage, so keep a copy somewhere else.
  • Importing or restoring a file reads a file you choose; nothing is uploaded.

App lock

If you set a PIN, it is stored on the phone as a salted hash, never as plain text. Fingerprint or face unlock is done by Android; the app does not receive your fingerprint or face data.

Purchases

The premium upgrade is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives only whether you own the upgrade, and remembers that on your phone. We do not receive or store your payment details or name. The app uses the internet only for this purchase check, and works fully without it.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share data.

Permissions

Internet and network state (only for Google Play purchases), biometric/fingerprint (only for the optional lock), and background-work permissions (to make the optional daily automatic backup on the phone). The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything from the phone, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Files you saved yourself (backups, exports) stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
